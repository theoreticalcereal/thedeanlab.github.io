Lab Policies
============

This section collects the recurring expectations that govern how the Dean Lab
works day to day. These pages are intended to clarify shared norms, not to
replace case-by-case discussion when judgment is needed.

At a glance
-----------

- Be present for recurring lab meetings unless you have arranged otherwise.
- Report vacation and sick leave in MyTime on time.
- Discuss authorship expectations early and revisit them as projects evolve.
- Keep shared lab spaces, samples, and work surfaces organized.
- Follow all UTSW safety, training, and waste-disposal requirements.
