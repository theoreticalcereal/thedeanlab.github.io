Lab Jobs
^^^^^^^^

Cleaning should be performed on a weekly basis. The person(s) responsible for the weekly lab jobs will be announced in Slack or placed on the Shared Outlook Calendar.

Responsibilities (weekly):

- Restock public supplies (pipettes, gloves, pipette tips, paper towels).
- Clean the floor and empty sharps and biohazard waste cans according to institutional procedures.
- Replace the dust mat.
- Wipe down the public bench in the optics lab.
- Ensure the top of the toolbox is clean and organized.

Notes
- Follow institutional safety and waste-disposal policies when handling and disposing of hazardous materials and sharps.
- If a job cannot be completed on the scheduled week, notify the lab and arrange coverage.
