Teaching
========

Teaching materials and short courses from the lab.

- `Intro-to-Python <https://github.com/TheDeanLab/Intro-to-Python>`_ - Introductory teaching materials for learning Python in a scientific research context.
- `CI-Nanocourse <https://github.com/TheDeanLab/CI-Nanocourse>`_ - Short-course materials covering continuous integration practices for research software.
- `dask-nanocourse <https://github.com/TheDeanLab/dask-nanocourse>`_ - Teaching materials for parallel and distributed computing with Dask.
